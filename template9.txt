PROJECT OVERVIEW
----------------
Objective:
  To analyze Olympic sports performance and present the results through
  interactive Tableau dashboards, embedded in a Flask website that also tells
  the story in four narrative points.

Scope:
  In scope:
  - Analysis views for medals by country (Top 10, breakdown, efficiency),
    medals over time, Summer vs. Winter totals, regional medal performance,
    athlete participation by sport, and athlete gender distribution (eight
    sheets reported in the Tableau workbook).
  - Two Tableau dashboards and one Tableau story published on Tableau Public.
  - A Flask website with a home page "/" and a "/story" page, deployed on
    Render and stored on GitHub.
  Out of scope:
  - Predictive models or forecasting.
  - A database or data warehouse (Snowflake is not used).
  - User accounts, login, or user data storage.
  - Automated data refresh (Not verified; not reported as implemented).

PROBLEM STATEMENT
-----------------
Description:
  Olympic performance data covers many countries, editions, sports, and medal
  types. In raw tables it is difficult for students and enthusiasts to compare
  countries, follow trends, or understand differences between Summer and
  Winter Games and between regions, and there is often no guided order to
  follow.

Impact:
  Solving this gives users a quick, visual, and ordered way to understand
  medal achievements and athlete participation. It also gives the student a
  complete example of a BI workflow: data in Tableau, dashboards published
  online, and a custom website around them. The real-world impact on users has
  Not been measured.

-----------------
Approach:
  1. Collect and load the Olympic datasets (Google Drive dataset folder) into
