Empathy Map Canvas:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
An empathy map is a simple, easy-to-digest visual that captures knowledge
about a user's behaviours and attitudes. It is a useful tool to help teams
better understand their users. Creating an effective solution requires
understanding the true problem and the person who is experiencing it. The
exercise of creating the map helps participants consider things from the
user's perspective along with his or her goals and challenges.
