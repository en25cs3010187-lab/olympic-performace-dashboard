PS-1
  I am (Customer)       : A student or sports enthusiast who wants to
                          understand how countries perform at the Olympics.
  I'm trying to         : Compare the medal achievements of countries and see
                          which ones lead overall.
  But                   : Raw medal data is hard to scan, rank, and compare
                          without charts.
  Because               : The data covers many countries, Olympic editions, and
                          medal types, so patterns are not obvious in rows and
                          columns.
  Which makes me feel   : Overwhelmed and unsure that my conclusions are right.

PS-2
  I am (Customer)       : A casual viewer (for example a classmate or reviewer)
                          who wants to follow the bigger picture of Olympic
                          performance.
  I'm trying to         : Understand trends over time, the difference between
                          Summer and Winter Games, and how regions compare.
  But                   : Separate charts do not tell me what to look at first
                          or why each one matters.
  Because               : There is no guided order or short explanation linking
                          the charts together.
  Which makes me feel   : Lost and less interested in exploring further.

How the project responds:
- PS-1 is addressed by the Tableau views Top 10 Countries by Medals, Medal
  Breakdown by Country, and Medal Efficiency by Country.
- PS-2 is addressed by the Flask /story page, which presents four narrative
  points: (1) Top 10 Countries by Medal Achievements, (2) Medal Performance
  Over Time, (3) Summer vs. Winter Olympics, (4) Regional Medal Performance.
